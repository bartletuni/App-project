TakomoCo - Capability Statement
TakomoCo is a specialized additive manufacturing and rapid prototyping studio. We focus on bridging the gap between digital design and physical reality, offering high-precision 3D printing and 3D scanning services for engineering and reproduction applications.
Core Competencies
Additive Manufacturing: Expert FDM/FFF printing with a focus on engineering-grade materials.
3D Scanning & Reverse Engineering: High-fidelity scanning for part reproduction and digital archiving.
Rapid Prototyping: Iterative design support to accelerate product development cycles.
•	Technical Material Expertise: Specializing in high-strength, chemically resistant, high temperature resistant, and impact resistant composites.
Technical Specifications
Maximum Build Volume: 220mm x 220mm x 220mm
Thermal Capacity: Optimized for materials requiring up to 280 deg C extrusion temperature.
•	Scanning Precision: Capable of capturing intricate geometries for near-exact 1:1 reproductions.
Material Specializations
We work with a wide array of high-performance thermoplastics, including but not limited to:
•	PPA-CF (Polyphthalamide Carbon Fiber)
•	Polycarbonate (PC)
•	PA6-CF/PA12-CF (Nylon - Carbon Fiber)
•	PETG, ASA, ABS, TPU, PLA, and specialized blends.
Differentiators
Material Mastery: Specialized in handling abrasive, carbon-fiber-reinforced materials.
End-to-End Workflow: From scanning a broken legacy part to delivering a fiber-reinforced replacement.
•	Agile Response: Small-scale focus allows for rapid pivots and personalized engineering support.
